Privacy

Last updated 2026-08-07

ja2-web lets you play Jagged Alliance 2 in a browser using your own copy of the game. This page describes exactly what the site stores and who else sees it. It is deliberately short, because the site does very little.

If you never sign in

Nothing is collected. There are no analytics, no tracking scripts and no advertising on this site — not Google Analytics, not anything else. If you use Bring your own JA2, your game files are read directly from your disk by your browser and are never uploaded; your saves are written back to a folder on your own machine.

If you use the Cloud Locker

Signing in is handled by Google, Discord or Microsoft. We never see your password. From them we receive, and store, only:

  • your email address and display name;
  • which provider you used, and an identifier from that provider;
  • the date the record was last updated.

We then store the game data you upload and your savegames, in storage private to your account. Nobody else can read them, and they are never public.

Where it is kept

On OVHcloud servers and object storage. Data is held for as long as your account exists. Ask us to delete it and we will remove your files and your account record.

Who else receives your data

  • Google, Discord or Microsoft — whichever you choose to sign in with.
  • Attio, a customer-records service: when you sign in, your email address and display name are sent there so we know who is using the site. Nothing about your gameplay, your saves or your files is sent.
  • Cloudflare, which sits in front of the site and processes requests (including your IP address) to serve and protect it.

Your data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Cookies

Two, both strictly necessary, neither used for tracking:

  • ja2_session — keeps you signed in. It is deleted when you close your browser, and expires within a day regardless.
  • ja2_oauth_state — a short-lived value that protects the sign-in itself. It lasts ten minutes.

Stored in your browser

Game files you have uploaded are cached locally so they need not be downloaded again. They stay on your machine and are cleared whenever you clear your browser’s site data.

Your choices

You can play without an account at all — use Bring your own JA2 and nothing leaves your machine. If you have signed in, you can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.
